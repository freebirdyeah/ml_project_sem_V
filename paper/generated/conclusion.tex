On the 205-circuit benchmark pool, our adaptive selector gave 0.73\% mean improvement over the best fixed training recipe with 95\% interval [0.25\%, 1.18\%]. It closed 9.20\% of the aggregate fixed-to-oracle objective gap. The confidence interval lies above zero, supporting an average gain on this circuit pool. Verifying five predicted candidates gave 5.16\% mean improvement and 3.95\% mean oracle regret. The five-recipe learned shortlist took 3.11 seconds per circuit including feature collection. The global eight-recipe shortlist reached 3.00\% regret in 3.18 seconds. For the tested runtime budgets, the global shortlist provided the stronger practical choice. The one-recipe result establishes a small circuit-specific selection benefit; the cost results favor spending this feature-collection overhead on additional candidate executions.
